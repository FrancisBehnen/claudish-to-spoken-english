Here is everything that changed in this pass:
- the retry logic moved out of the hook and into the provider layer where it belongs
- the timeout is now a watchdog that sends TERM and then KILL rather than a bare wait
- the off switch is checked fresh on every message instead of once at launch
- the notice fires only once per session and never suppresses any content
- the buffers are swept opportunistically rather than on a timer
- the recursion guard is an environment variable that both hooks read early
- the message travels on standard input so its length is never capped
- nothing in the fail open path can swallow or stall the assistant answer
