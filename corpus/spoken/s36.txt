The provisioning run settled every open question about footprint.
Resident memory plateaued at about 665 MB under sustained load.
It dropped to roughly 380 MB shortly after going idle.
Genuinely idle, it sat as low as 28 MB with no measurable CPU.
Memory stopped growing by the fifth repetition and did not leak.
It survived two sleep and wake cycles without leaking either.
The only cost was the first synthesis after a wake, at 4.9 seconds.
